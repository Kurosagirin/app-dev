---

## 📖 Synopsis
Set in a near-future Kyoto, a shy high schooler named **Naomi Katagaki** meets a mysterious man who claims to be his **future self**.  
He reveals that Naomi must save his classmate and first love, **Ruri Ichigyō**, from an impending tragedy.  
As Naomi learns to alter time and reality, he discovers the deeper consequences of rewriting fate and the fragile boundaries between memory and simulation.

## 🎨 Production Notes
- Directed by **Tomohiko Itō**, known for *Sword Art Online*.  
- Script by **Mado Nozaki**, author of *Babylon*.  
- Produced by **Graphinica**, blending 3D CG and 2D animation to create a cinematic, futuristic Kyoto.  
- The film emphasizes digital landscapes, light particles, and symbolic color motifs (red = reality, blue = data).

---

## 🗓️ Release & Reception
- **Premiere:** September 11, 2019 (Kyoto)  
- **Japan Release:** September 20, 2019  
- **Distributor:** Toho  
- **Box Office:** Earned several million worldwide  
- **Reception:**  
  - Praised for its **visuals**, **music**, and **conceptual storytelling**.  
  - Mixed reviews for pacing and narrative complexity.

## 📚 Related Media
- **Novelization:** Written by Mado Nozaki.  
- **Manga Adaptations:** Two manga series expanding different parts of the story.  
- **Spin-off Anime:** *HELLO WORLD: Another World* — a 3-episode mini-anime focusing on future Naomi’s perspective.

## 💡 Themes
- The ethics of time manipulation and rewriting the past.  
- The interplay between memory, digital existence, and emotion.  
- Questions of identity in simulated and real worlds.  
- A metaphor for youth, regret, and the drive to fix mistakes.
